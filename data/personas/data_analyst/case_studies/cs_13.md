# Case Study: Patient No-Show Rate Reduction & Scheduling Optimization (2025, Healthcare)

## Context
In 2025, I engaged with a large enterprise healthcare provider to address a critical operational inefficiency: high no-show rates for follow-up appointments. The clinic was losing approximately 15% of scheduled slots weekly, resulting in wasted clinician time and reduced capacity for new patient intake. As the Data Analyst, I was tasked with identifying root causes and implementing a data-driven intervention to stabilize appointment fill rates.

## Challenge
The primary challenge was the lack of a unified view of patient appointment history across multiple departmental databases. Data was siloed, making it difficult to correlate demographic factors, appointment type, and historical behavior. Additionally, the existing reminder system was static—sending a generic email 48 hours before every appointment regardless of patient risk. This approach lacked granularity and failed to prioritize high-risk patients who required more intensive outreach.

## Approach
I began by consolidating six months of appointment data into a centralized BigQuery warehouse. Using SQL, I joined demographic, clinical, and scheduling tables to create a comprehensive dataset of 45,000+ appointments. I then performed exploratory data analysis in Python using pandas to identify patterns. Key findings revealed that patients residing further from the clinic and those with a history of missed initial appointments had the highest no-show probabilities.

I developed a logistic regression model using statsmodels to assign a risk score (0-100) to each upcoming appointment. This model allowed the care team to segment patients into low, medium, and high-risk categories. Based on these scores, I designed a tiered communication strategy: low-risk patients received standard automated emails, while high-risk patients triggered both SMS and email reminders 72 hours and 24 hours prior to their visit.

## Stack
*   **Data Storage & Querying:** Google BigQuery, SQL
*   **Data Processing & Modeling:** Python (pandas, statsmodels, google-cloud-bigquery)
*   **Orchestration & Automation:** Apache Airflow
*   **Visualization:** Power BI (for stakeholder reporting)

## Results
The implementation of the risk-based reminder system yielded measurable improvements within the first quarter of deployment. The overall no-show rate for follow-up appointments decreased by 22%, significantly outperforming the previous static reminder baseline. Concurrently, the appointment fill rate increased by 5%, translating to an estimated 120 additional patient visits per month. This increase in capacity allowed the clinic to reduce wait times for new patients by approximately three days. The automated Airflow pipeline ensured that reminder triggers were consistent and error-free, reducing manual administrative overhead for scheduling staff.

## What I would do differently for this project
While the initial model provided strong baseline performance, I would have integrated real-time data sources to further refine risk scoring. Specifically, incorporating last-minute traffic data or weather conditions could have improved accuracy for patients with mobility challenges. Additionally, I would have implemented an A/B testing framework earlier in the project to compare different reminder copy and timing strategies. This would have allowed for more granular optimization of the communication channels, potentially yielding an even higher reduction in no-shows. Finally, I would have collaborated more closely with the clinical team during the model development phase to ensure that the risk factors aligned with their operational constraints and patient interaction protocols.
