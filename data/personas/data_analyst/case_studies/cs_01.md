# Churn Prediction & Cohort Retention Dashboard for SaaS Startups (2023)

**Context**  
In early 2023, a SaaS startup with 52,000 active users faced accelerating churn—22% monthly—despite strong acquisition pipelines. The leadership team needed actionable insights to prevent revenue loss without increasing support overhead.

**Challenge**  
The CTO required identifying high-risk user segments within 30 days of onboarding to prioritize targeted support interventions. Existing analytics tools provided only aggregate churn rates, lacking segmentation granularity for timely outreach.

**Approach**  
I engineered a production-ready pipeline in 6 weeks using dbt-core to transform 10M+ daily event logs into 42 behavioral features (e.g., session frequency, feature adoption velocity, support ticket patterns).
