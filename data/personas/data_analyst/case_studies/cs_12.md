# Engineering Log: Donor Lifecycle & Fundraising Efficiency Dashboard (2022, Non-profit)

**Project ID:** p12
**Role:** Freelance Analyst
**Duration:** 3 Weeks (2022)

## Phase 1: Data Ingestion & Normalization (Week 1)

*   **Source Audit:** Identified 4 disparate data sources: legacy Excel spreadsheets (2018–2020), Salesforce CRM exports, bank transaction CSVs, and event ticketing logs.
*   **Schema Design:** Designed a star schema in PostgreSQL to normalize donation events. Created `donors`, `campaigns`, `donations`, and `channels` dimensions. Fact table `fct_donations` grain: one row per donation event.
*   **ETL Scripting:** Built Python ETL pipeline using `pandas` for initial cleaning and `sqlalchemy` for database connectivity.
    *   *Decision:* Rejected raw Excel imports due to inconsistent date formats (`MM/DD/YYYY` vs `DD-MM-YY`). Standardized all timestamps to `UTC ISO 8601`.
    *   *Issue:* 12% of records in the 2019 export had missing `donor_id`. Resolved by fuzzy matching on email address (78% recovery rate).
*   **RFM Implementation:** Calculated Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores in SQL.
    *   *Logic:* `Recency` = Days since last donation (as of 2022-06-30). `Frequency` = Count of donations in trailing 24 months. `Monetary` = Sum of donation amounts.
    *   *Segmentation:* Binned scores into quintiles (1-5) for cohort analysis.

## Phase 2: Data Modeling & Power BI Development (Week 2)

*   **Semantic Model:** Connected Power BI Desktop to PostgreSQL via ODBC. Created calculated tables for cohort retention matrices.
*   **DAX Measures:**
    *   `Donor Lifetime Value (LTV)`: `SUM(fct_donations[amount]) / COUNT(DISTINCT fct_donations[donor_id])` per cohort.
    *   `Repeat Rate`: `DIVIDE(COUNTROWS(FILTER(fct_donations, fct_donations[frequency] > 1)), COUNTROWS(fct_donations))`.
*   **Visuals Built:**
    1.  **Cohort Heatmap:** Tracks retention % for donors acquired by month (Jan 2020 – Dec 2021).
    2.  **RFM Scatter Plot:** X-axis = Recency, Y-axis = Frequency, Bubble Size = Monetary. Color-coded by primary acquisition channel.
    3.  **Channel Efficiency Bar Chart:** Compares Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) vs. 12-Month Retention Rate for Email, Social, Direct Mail, and Events.
*   **Performance Tuning:** Initial refresh time was 14 minutes. Optimized by pre-aggregating monthly totals in SQL instead of calculating in DAX. Reduced refresh time to 4 minutes.

## Phase 3: Validation & Deployment (Week 3)

*   **Statistical Validation:** Performed chi-square test to determine if channel differences in retention were statistically significant.
    *   *Result:* p-value = 0.03. Email marketing showed significantly higher 12-month retention (34%) compared to Direct Mail (18%).
*   **Client Review:** Presented findings to Executive Director.
    *   *Key Insight:* 40% of budget was allocated to Direct Mail, which had the lowest LTV contribution.
    *   *Recommendation:* Shift 20% of Q3 budget from Direct Mail to Email and Social retargeting.
*   **Outcome Tracking:**
    *   Implemented A/B test framework in Power BI to monitor post-change performance.
    *   Projected annual savings: $15,000 based on reduced printing/postage costs for low-performing mailers.
    *   Observed 10% increase in repeat donation rate within the first 60 days of reallocation (hypothesis: better targeting via RFM segmentation).

## Technical Notes & Decisions

*   **Library Stack:** `pandas` v1.4.2, `sqlalchemy` v1.4.37, Power BI Desktop v2.120.
*   **Data Quality:** Added data quality checks in Python script (null checks, range validation for donation amounts). Logged 45 anomalies to `dq_issues` table.
*   **Access Control:** Restricted `donor_id` and `email` fields in Power BI to "Admin" role only due to GDPR/PII concerns. General staff see aggregated metrics only.
*   **Documentation:** Created 15-page user guide with definitions for RFM scoring and LTV calculation. No public repository created due to sensitive donor data.
