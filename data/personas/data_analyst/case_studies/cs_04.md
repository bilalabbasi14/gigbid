# Real-Time Ad Spend Optimization Dashboard

**Client Industry:** Media Agency
**Year:** 2022

### Context
In early 2022, I was contracted by a mid-sized media agency managing paid social and search campaigns for over twenty consumer brands. The agency’s primary pain point was the significant lag between ad spend occurrence and performance reporting. Media buyers were manually exporting raw data from Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, and TikTok Ads Manager, then pasting it into individual Excel workbooks. This process was not only time-consuming but also prone to human error, such as formula breaks or incorrect date ranges, which compromised the accuracy of client-facing reports.

### Challenge
The core challenge was eliminating the manual data transfer bottleneck while maintaining a low-latency view of campaign performance. The agency needed to move from end-of-day reporting to near-real-time visibility to allow media buyers to adjust bids and pausing underperforming assets during the day. Additionally, the existing reporting structure lacked a unified view of cost-per-acquisition (CPA) and click-through rate (CTR) anomalies across different platforms, making it difficult to identify inefficient spend quickly.

### Approach
I designed a lightweight automation pipeline to centralize data ingestion and visualization. First, I developed a Python script that authenticated with the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) API and respective ad platform APIs. This script executed every 15 minutes, pulling key metrics including spend, impressions, clicks, and conversions. Using `pandas` and `numpy`, I cleaned and aggregated the data, calculating derived metrics like CTR and CPA. The cleaned dataset was then appended to a central Google Sheet via the `gspread` library, serving as a single source of truth. Finally, I connected this Google Sheet to Tableau, building a dashboard that highlighted anomalies in CTR and CPA using reference lines and conditional formatting.

### Stack
*   **Language:** Python
*   **Libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `gspread`, `openpyxl`
*   **Data Sources:** Google Analytics 4, Google Ads API, Meta Graph API
*   **Visualization:** Tableau
*   **Storage:** Google Sheets

### Results
The implementation delivered immediate operational improvements. By automating the data pull, the agency saved approximately 10 hours per week previously spent on manual data entry and reconciliation. More importantly, the 15-minute refresh frequency enabled media buyers to monitor spend efficiency in near-real-time. This allowed for faster decision-making, such as pausing ads with unexpectedly high CPAs within the hour of occurrence. The unified Tableau dashboard reduced the time required to generate client reports from several days to under an hour, as the data was already centralized and formatted.

### What I would do differently for this project
While the Google Sheets-based solution was effective for the agency’s scale and timeline, I would have considered migrating the data warehouse to a cloud-native solution like BigQuery for future scalability. Google Sheets has row limits and can become a performance bottleneck as data volume increases. Additionally, I would have integrated a more robust alerting system directly within the pipeline, using email or Slack notifications for critical anomalies, rather than relying solely on visual detection in Tableau. This would have provided an extra layer of proactivity for media buyers who might not have the dashboard open at all times.
